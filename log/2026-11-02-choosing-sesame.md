# 2026-11-02 · Picking a first robot: Sesame

**Tags:** build

Decision made: my first robot will be [Sesame](https://github.com/dorianborian/sesame-robot), a small ESP32 quadruped, instead of the SO-101 arm kit.

The SO-101 is great, but it comes as a kit. I wanted something more hands-on that I could build step by step: solder the electronics, print the parts, wire the servos, write the firmware, and break things along the way.
